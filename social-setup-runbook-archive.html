⚠Will-get-you-banned list

These behaviours flag new accounts for shadow-bans, demotion, or outright suspension. Avoid all of them in week one:

  1. Mass-follow. Following more than 50 accounts in a single day on a brand-new profile triggers Instagram's automation flag. Spread out follows. Target 10-20/day max for the first month.
  2. Follow / unfollow cycles. Following someone, waiting for them to follow back, then unfollowing — instant shadow-ban if detected.
  3. Bot tools and engagement pods. Anything that automates likes/follows/comments. Instagram has gotten extremely good at detecting these. Lifetime ban risk on the account.
  4. Buying followers. Bought followers are bots with no engagement → Instagram sees a high follower count with low engagement-per-follower ratio and assumes the account is inauthentic → suppresses reach to real followers too.
  5. Identical captions across posts. Posting the same caption multiple times triggers spam flags. Each post needs a unique caption.
  6. 30 hashtags per post. Instagram now flags hashtag-stuffing (more than 15). Use 5-10 specific hashtags per post.
  7. Paid ads in week one. Meta's ad delivery models perform terribly on accounts with no organic engagement signal. Wait 30 days before running ads.
  8. Identical content cross-posted unedited. If you post the same image and caption on Instagram and Facebook within 60 seconds, Meta detects and demotes both. Stagger by 1-2 hours, OR change one element (caption, hashtags).
  9. Auto-DM bots welcoming new followers. Bans common in 2024-2026. Send personal welcomes by hand for the first 100 followers, then re-evaluate.
  10. Reposting other people's content without credit. Even with credit, repost-only profiles get suppressed. Original content only for at least the first six months.
  11. Adding banned hashtags. Some hashtags are "shadow-banned" by Instagram (no posts using them appear in searches). Avoid #spirituality (often flagged), #psychic, #astrology, #reading. Use specific ones: #AkashicRecords, #AnimalCommunicator, #RainbowBridge.
  12. Logging in from multiple locations rapidly. If Ruchita's account is logged in from Ahmedabad and someone (Sagar) also logs in from a different city minutes later, Instagram flags it as a hack attempt. Coordinate logins, or only Sagar uses Meta Business Suite, not the IG app itself.
